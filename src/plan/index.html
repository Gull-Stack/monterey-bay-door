The upside: money you stop leaving on the table

Three places the HQ pays for itself. We only used published prices. Where the real number depends on you, we say so.

1. Big invoices, tiny fees

You told us your card processor added fees after two months. In the HQ, a customer can pay a big invoice by bank transfer through your own Stripe account: 0.8%, and never more than $5. Try your own number.

Stripe's published bank-transfer price is 0.8%, capped at $5. Card rates vary. Send us one recent processor statement and we'll show your real rate, hidden fees included.
